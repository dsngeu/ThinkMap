A topic is one thing you discuss and build: a feature, a decision area, a subsystem.
Everything known about it goes into the six buckets below. Delete this file once you
have real topics, or rename it and keep the examples as a reference.

## Points
- [discussed 2026-10-29] P1-F01-01: Walked through what this topic needs to do
  > What: A record that the team talked it through — who needs it, rough scope.
  > Discussed: Two approaches were compared; the simpler one is preferred for v1.

- [decided 2026-10-29] P1-F01-02: Example decision — keep the first version small
  > What: We build the smallest version that is useful and extend it later.
  > Why: Faster feedback; large first versions are usually wrong in the details.
  > Expected: Version 1 ships in one phase; extensions are new points in this topic.
  > Built: Not yet — ticket P1-T01.

- [open 2026-10-29] P1-F01-03: Example open question — which option do we pick?
  > What: A question nobody has answered yet.
  > Options: (a) The simple option. (b) The flexible but slower option.
  > Next: The owner decides; then change `open` → `decided` and write `Why:`.

- [suggestion 2026-10-29 claude] P1-F01-04: Example idea waiting for a yes/no
  > What: An idea proposed (usually by Claude) that nobody has accepted yet.
  > Why: Cheap to add, clear benefit.
  > Next: Accept → `decided`. Reject → `dropped` with `Why:`.

- [caution 2026-10-29] P1-F01-05: Example rule to remember while building
  > What: A rule or risk that must not be forgotten.
  > Why: Explain what it protects.
  > Risk: What goes wrong if it is ignored.

- [dropped 2026-10-29] P1-F01-06: Example idea we decided not to do
  > What: Something considered and rejected. Keep the line so nobody re-proposes it.
  > Why: The reason it was rejected.
